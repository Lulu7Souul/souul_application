# Souul — Raw Product Brief (Visionary Input)
> Captured: 2026-04-08. Source: ChatGPT-distilled product architecture output.

## One-line product definition
A web-first, adult-authored, child-played guided-sequence platform that uses visual structure plus optional music/rhyme cues to support routines, transitions, learning, social preparation, communication, and co-regulation.

## North Star
Reduce friction and repeated adult prompting while increasing:
- participation
- independence
- help-seeking
- communication
- smoother transitions
- shared coordination across home, school, and therapy

## Core Product Object: Guided Sequence
One reusable unit. Can be: routine / transition / social prep story / learning/practice / calm-down.

Each sequence contains: goal, steps, visuals, optional rhyme/music/voice cue per step, timer/pacing cue, help path, communication supports, completion state, optional celebration/reward, session log.

## Three Surfaces
1. **Author** — parent, carer, therapist, educator creates/edits sequences
2. **Player** — child-facing mode, runs one sequence at a time, almost no branching
3. **Review** — adults see friction points, skipped steps, timing patterns

## Three Pillars
- **Predictability** — visual order, first-then, timers, what-happens-next
- **Expression** — music/rhyme cues, voice prompts, choice boards, communication cards, story support
- **Coordination** — shared authoring, team roles, insights, cross-context consistency

## Music/Rhyme Scope
Step-bound, optional, profile-specific, easy to disable.
5 cue types only: start / transition / pacing / celebration / calm.

## V1 Scope (Must Have)
- Profile setup
- Guided-sequence builder
- Routine + story + calm sequence types
- Custom image/audio upload
- Music/rhyme cue attachment
- First-then and choice boards
- Child player
- Basic insights
- Team sharing (one additional adult role)
- Privacy/consent/admin basics

## V1 Cut List (Keep Out)
- Full AAC system
- Open social/community features
- AI-generated therapy claims
- Biometric regulation hardware
- Giant content marketplace
- Dense gamification economy
- Diagnosis-specific scoring
- Child-facing chat or feed

## Design Guardrails
- One step per child screen
- Large touch targets
- Minimal reading requirement
- Adult settings never appear in child mode
- Every sequence includes Help, More time, Break
- Rewards optional, not primary motivation model
- Analytics track friction/adaptation, not "good/bad child" judgment
- Music can be turned off per child, per sequence, or per step
- No ads. No child-facing dark patterns. No unnecessary data collection.

## Key Product Position
Neuroinclusive support for participation, independence, communication, co-regulation.
NOT an autism platform. NOT a music app.
NOT competing with Proloquo2Go or Mightier.
Differentiated from Choiceworks/Goally/Tiimo by: music/rhyme as a structural cue layer.

## Competitive Landscape Referenced
Choiceworks, Goally, Nelli, Jam Up, Proloquo2Go, Pictello, Tiimo, Brili, Mightier, Otsimo.

## Governing Frameworks
- Structured teaching / visual supports
- Social Stories (Carol Gray criteria)
- AAC / presume competence / personalization
- UDL + cognitive accessibility + co-design
- Child safety and privacy by design (COPPA, GDPR-K)
