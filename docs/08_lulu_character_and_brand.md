# Lulu — Character, Brand & Name Decision
> Status: LOCKED. Date: 2026-04-08.

---

## The Decision

**Lulu** is both the product name and the in-app companion guide.

They are one and the same — the product IS Lulu. This is intentional and powerful.

> "Meet Lulu. She's your child's calm guide."

This is stronger than having a separate brand name + a character name. The product and the companion are unified. The child doesn't use "an app" — they spend time with Lulu.

---

## What Lulu Is

Lulu is a **calm guide character** who lives inside the product.

She is not:
- A cartoon mascot (too stimulating, too childish)
- A chatbot or voice assistant (too complex, too AI-feeling)
- A teacher or authority figure (wrong relationship)

She is:
- A warm presence — always there, always calm
- The voice that guides children through their steps
- The face that celebrates when they're done
- The quiet companion during a calm-down moment
- Predictable — children know what Lulu will do

**Lulu's character in three words:** Warm. Steady. Yours.

---

## How Lulu Appears in the Product

### In the child player:
- Lulu's avatar (simple, warm, non-overwhelming — not a detailed cartoon)
- Lulu's voice = the voice cues on each step (parent records as Lulu, or uses bundled sounds)
- "Lulu says..." appears as the optional help text framing
- Lulu celebrates with the child on completion
- Lulu is present in the calm corner — gentle, quiet, reassuring

### In the adult workspace:
- Lulu is present but subtle — the product personality shows through tone of copy
- "Lulu is ready for [child's name]" — dashboard state
- Sequence templates are described as "Lulu's morning routine" etc.
- Lulu's voice can be customised (parent records their own voice as Lulu's voice for the child)

### In marketing:
- The product is Lulu. "Lulu for families."
- Not "an app called Lulu" — just Lulu.
- Lulu speaks directly to parents in copy: warm, calm, never clinical.

## Lulu's Visual Identity Principles

### Character design direction:
- Abstract enough to not overwhelm — think soft shape, not detailed face
- Warm colour (from brand palette — brand-500 green or calm purple)
- Consistent across all screens — children need to recognise Lulu instantly
- Works at tiny sizes (favicon, notification icon) and large sizes (celebration screen)
- No gender implied by design — Lulu is universal

### Options for character form (to be decided with a designer):
1. **Simple face** — two eyes, no mouth, gentle — like a cloud or pebble with warmth
2. **Abstract shape** — a soft rounded form that pulses gently on celebrations
3. **No character face** — Lulu is represented only by colour, sound, and name (cleanest, no risk of "wrong" character feel)

**PO recommendation: Start with option 3 (no face) for V1.** Add a face in V2 once we've done co-design with families and children.

## Lulu's Voice & Tone (Copy Guidelines)

**Lulu speaks like:** A warm, calm childminder who genuinely loves what they do.

**In the child player:**
- No long sentences. One idea per screen.
- Warm but not gushing. "Well done." not "AMAZING SUPERSTAR!!!"
- Predictable phrasing — children need to know what's coming

**In the adult product:**
- Direct, warm, never clinical
- Acknowledges the parent's reality: "Mornings can be hard. Lulu helps."
- Never preachy about neurodivergence. Never diagnostic.

**Never:**
- "Your autistic child"
- "Therapy-grade routines"
- "Proven by science to..."
- "Fix", "correct", "normal", "improve behaviour"

**Always:**
- "Your child"
- "Routines that work for your family"
- "Lulu helps [child's name] know what's coming next"
